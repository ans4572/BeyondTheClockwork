Redraw six HUD icons for Beyond the Clockwork, a cute wind-up toy-town roguelite, as SMOOTH illustrated icons instead of pixel art.
They are interface, not world art: they sit on the cream HUD tags and buttons at the top of the screen (the wallet tag at the top left,
three square buttons at the top right), shown at about 22-28 screen pixels, and up to 56 pixels on a phone or a 4K screen.
The characters and maps stay pixel art; only these UI icons become smooth.

REFERENCES
- art\ui\icons\gear-v1.png, coin-v1.png, map-v1.png, settings-v1.png, fullscreen-v1.png, fullscreen-exit-v1.png:
  the current pixel versions. Keep each one's idea and silhouette; redraw it smoothly.
- art\chapters\1-smile-street\maps\00-village-court\background-wide-v7.png: the game's painted town. Take its warm brass, cream and
  crimson materials and soft top-left sunlight from here.
Palette: navy ink #2b3345 for outlines and line icons, polished brass and gold (#d9a441, #f3cd6a highlight, #9a6a24 shade),
warm cream #f6ecd2, a touch of crimson #c8463c. The buttons behind them are cream #f6ecd2 with a navy edge.

STYLE (all six the same)
- Clean vector-like illustration: smooth anti-aliased edges, crisp curves, no visible pixels, no pixel grid, no dithering.
- One consistent navy outline, about 1/16 of the icon width, rounded joins. Simple two-tone shading (base + one shade) with
  one small soft highlight top-left. No gradients wider than that, no texture, no noise.
- Bold and simple so it still reads at 22 pixels: one silhouette, at most 4-5 colours, no tiny details.
- Square 512x512 PNG, TRANSPARENT background, the object centred with about 40 px of empty margin on every side.
- No text, letters, numbers, button frame, drop shadow outside the object, glow halo, watermark or characters.
