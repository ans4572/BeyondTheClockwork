Redraw the DODGE (회피) icon for Beyond the Clockwork, a cute wind-up toy-town roguelite. It is the quick dash: the last slot of the skill bar and the dodge button on phones, shown at 36-48 screen pixels next to the other skill icons.

WHY A REDRAW
- art\ui\icons\action-dodge-v3.png (the current one): the shoe is good, but its heat jet was drawn by a script: smooth geometric bands of flat colour and perfect circles. Beside the hand-made icons it looks code-drawn, not pixel-painted. Keep the IDEA, redraw the whole icon by hand.

KEEP FROM v3
- A brass wind-up toy shoe seen from the side, pointing right, tilted slightly forward: brass body, rounded cream toe cap, cream sole, short teal laces (#3f9a8c), one brass rivet, a small brass wind-up key on the back of the ankle.
- A round exhaust vent at the heel, glowing inside, blasting hot air to the left that pushes the shoe forward.

STYLE REFERENCES (match these exactly)

- art\ui\icons\skill-pressureDash-v1.png, art\ui\icons\skill-exhaustBurst-v1.png, art\ui\icons\skill-heatSwing-v1.png: the new skill icons; their flames and wind are the way to draw the exhaust.
Match their crisp hand-placed pixel clusters, 1 grid pixel dark navy outline (#2b3345), top-left light, saturation and pixel density.
Palette: polished brass and gold, warm cream, a touch of teal on the laces, warm orange (#e8742f) to pale yellow (#ffe086) to white for the exhaust, navy ink outline (#2b3345).

THE EXHAUST, DRAWN BY HAND
- Two or three curling puffs of hot air and steam, of different sizes, overlapping, each with its own navy outline and a lighter top-left edge, like the smoke in skill-exhaustBurst-v1.png.
- Hottest (orange, then yellow) right at the vent, cooling to cream and white puffs toward the tail; one or two small loose wisps or sparks behind.
- No smooth gradient bands, no perfect geometric circles or cones, no straight speed lines.
- The shoe fills the right two thirds of the frame, the exhaust the left third, the whole icon one bold silhouette that reads as "quick dash" at 24 pixels.

DRAW IT
- Pixel art on a 48x48 pixel grid, then output at 384x384 with every grid pixel as an exact 8x8 block (no smoothing, no anti-aliasing, no half pixels), so it scales down to 48x48 perfectly.
- TRANSPARENT background (PNG with alpha), centred, 2 grid pixels of empty margin.
- At most 10 colours.

RULES
- No text, letters, numbers or shapes that read as letters or digits.
- No flames tall enough to read as a fire skill, no wings, clock, coils, legs, characters, slot frame, background tile or watermark.
