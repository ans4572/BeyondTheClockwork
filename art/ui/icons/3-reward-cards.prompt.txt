Create the REWARD CARD ICONS for Beyond the Clockwork, a cute wind-up toy-town roguelite. After each fight the player picks one of three reward cards; each card shows one of these icons in a 56x56 tile at the top of the card. They also appear smaller (24-32 pixels) in lists.

REFERENCES (style, palette, pixel density)
- art\ui\skill-icons\heatSwing.png, art\ui\skill-icons\frostBolt.png, art\ui\skill-icons\conductiveSpanner.png (48x48): the game's own pixel-art skill icons, shown on the same cards. Match their crisp pixel clusters, dark navy outline, shading and saturation exactly so the new icons sit beside them.
- art\ui\icons\gear-v1.png and art\ui\icons\coin-v1.png (if they exist): the currency icons from the same pass.
Palette: polished brass and gold, warm cream, navy ink outline (#2b3345), crimson, mint, sky blue; gentle top-left light.

DRAW EACH ICON
- Pixel art on a 48x48 pixel grid, then output at 384x384 with every grid pixel as an exact 8x8 block (no smoothing, no anti-aliasing, no half pixels), so it scales down to 48x48 perfectly.
- TRANSPARENT background (PNG with alpha), one centred object, 2 grid pixels of empty margin, a 1 grid pixel dark navy outline.
- Must read at 28 pixels: one bold silhouette, at most 8-10 colours, no text.
- Every icon must have a clearly different silhouette from the others.
