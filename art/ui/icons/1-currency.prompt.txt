Create the CURRENCY ICONS for Beyond the Clockwork, a cute wind-up toy-town roguelite. They sit in the HUD next to a number (e.g. "120"), on reward cards and in shop prices, usually shown at 20-32 screen pixels.

REFERENCES (style, palette, pixel density)
- art\ui\skill-icons\heatSwing.png and art\ui\skill-icons\frostBolt.png (48x48): the game's own pixel-art icons. Match their crisp pixel clusters, dark navy outline and warm palette.
- art\ui\element-icons\fire.png (32x32): the smaller icon size these must also read at.
- art\world-map\icons\boss-marker-v2.png: the brass, cream and crimson materials of the game's newest markers.
Palette: polished brass and gold, warm cream, navy ink outline (#2b3345), a touch of crimson; gentle top-left light.

DRAW EACH ICON
- Pixel art on a 32x32 pixel grid, then output at 256x256 with every grid pixel as an exact 8x8 block (no smoothing, no anti-aliasing, no half pixels), so it scales down to 32x32 perfectly.
- TRANSPARENT background (PNG with alpha). One centred object, 1-2 grid pixels of empty margin. A 1 grid pixel dark navy outline around the silhouette.
- Must read instantly at 20 pixels: one bold silhouette, at most 6-8 colours, no tiny details or text.
