Draw 8 FLOOR MARKS for Beyond the Clockwork, a cute wind-up toy-town roguelite seen from a 3/4 top-down camera. They replace plain code circles: where a skill is about to land, where a crate is about to blow, where an enemy is about to climb out, the toothed ring under an elite's feet. The game turns, pulses, fades and squashes them itself.

HOW EVERY MARK IS DRAWN
- Seen from STRAIGHT ABOVE, a perfect circle (not an ellipse); the game squashes it onto the floor.
- Pixel art on the grid given, output with every grid pixel as an exact square block (no smoothing, no anti-aliasing, no half pixels).
- TRANSPARENT background (PNG with alpha); fully opaque pixels only (the game fades them).
- Mostly an open ring and a few marks inside, so the floor and the bodies on it still show through.
- No text, letters, numbers, characters, shadows or background tile.
