Draw 8 WARNING GLYPHS for Beyond the Clockwork, a cute wind-up toy-town roguelite. They float over a boss's or an enemy's attack zone for a second before it hits, replacing letters like 吸, 쿵, 종, 틈 and !. Shown at about 18 screen pixels over a red-hatched floor zone, so each must read instantly at that size.

Palette: warning red #d8423c / #b85863, cream #fff4d6, brass #c79a3e / #ffd257, navy ink #2b3345. At most 6 colours each.

DRAW EACH GLYPH
- Pixel art on a 24x24 grid, output at 384x384 with every grid pixel as an exact 16x16 block (no smoothing, no anti-aliasing, no half pixels), so it scales down to 24x24 perfectly.
- TRANSPARENT background (PNG with alpha), one centred bold shape, 1 grid pixel of empty margin, navy outline round the silhouette.
- Must read at 16 pixels. No text, letters or numbers.
